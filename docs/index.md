# Mixed models. *Built to scale.*

OPEN-SOURCE R SOFTWARE

sommer is an R package for advanced mixed models, quantitative genetics,
genomic prediction, G×E analysis, and large-scale statistical computing.

WHAT SOMMER DOES

## One framework for complex models.

G

### Quantitative genetics

Variance components, relationship matrices, heritability, BLUPs, and
advanced genetic models.

×

### G×E analysis

Model genotype-by-environment interaction with flexible covariance
structures and heterogeneous variances.

Σ

### Multivariate models

Fit multiple traits and structured covariance relationships within a
unified mixed-model framework.

∾

### Genomic prediction

Work with genomic relationship matrices and modern prediction workflows
in R.

⊥

### Sparse computation

Designed for computationally demanding mixed-model problems where matrix
structure matters.

R

### R-native workflow

Formula-based modeling, familiar R data structures, and an ecosystem
built for reproducible research.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

## Need help with a difficult model?

sommer is free and open source. Professional consulting is available for
organizations that need expert help implementing, optimizing, or
extending advanced mixed-model and quantitative-genetic analyses.

### Advanced mixed models

Model development for multivariate, G×E, spatial, heterogeneous, and
structured covariance problems.

### Performance optimization

Profile and accelerate large mixed-model workflows, sparse computations,
and demanding genomic analyses.

### Custom development

New covariance structures, algorithms, R/C++ functionality, and research
software tailored to a scientific problem.

### Genomic prediction

Design and implement quantitative-genetic and genomic prediction
workflows for research and breeding programs.

### Migration & integration

Help move established mixed-model workflows into reproducible R and
sommer-based pipelines.

### Training & workshops

Practical training in mixed models, quantitative genetics, genomic
prediction, and advanced sommer workflows.
