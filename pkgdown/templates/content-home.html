WHAT SOMMER DOES

One framework for complex models.

G

Quantitative genetics

Variance components, relationship matrices, heritability, BLUPs, and advanced genetic models.

×

G×E analysis

Model genotype-by-environment interaction with flexible covariance structures and heterogeneous variances.

Σ

Multivariate models

Fit multiple traits and structured covariance relationships within a unified mixed-model framework.

∾

Genomic prediction

Work with genomic relationship matrices and modern prediction workflows in R.

⊥

Sparse computation

Designed for computationally demanding mixed-model problems where matrix structure matters.

R

R-native workflow

Formula-based modeling, familiar R data structures, and an ecosystem built for reproducible research.
